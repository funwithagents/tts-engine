# Output gain: `gain_db` on the engine block

**Status:** Done

Implements [specs/configuration.md](../specs/configuration.md) ("`engine.gain_db`", "Validation rules"), [specs/architecture.md](../specs/architecture.md) ("Output gain") and [specs/voice-audition.md](../specs/voice-audition.md) ("Level and gain"). One optional, static gain per engine, applied to every PCM chunk between the module and the sink, so a consumer playing several engines through one output can level them; the audition tool measures each voice's level and suggests the value. It deliberately leaves out a limiter (hard clip plus a warning), a per-call gain, LUFS, and rejecting unknown engine-block keys, all recorded as open questions.

## Scope

- `src/tts_engine/config.py` — `TTSEngineConfig.gain_db: float = 0.0`, validated by `from_dict` (finite `int`/`float`, not `bool`, −60 to +24).
- `src/tts_engine/engine.py` — private `_GainSink` wrapping the resolved sink when `gain_db != 0`: per-chunk scale, round, clip; clip count logged once per utterance in `drain`; an odd-length chunk raises `ValueError`.
- `tests/test_config.py` — default, every constructor, accepted values, rejected values.
- `tests/test_engine.py` — on the `tone` fixture: absent and `0` bit-exact, −6.02 dB halves the samples in `say` and `say_stream` and keeps the module's chunking, the default player gets it too, a clipping gain saturates and warns once per utterance, a non-clipping gain does not warn, an odd chunk raises.
- `tools/voice-audition/audition.py`, `page.html` — `level_dbfs` and `peak_dbfs` in `ok` results (filled in from the WAV for older entries), `--target` on `render` and `page`, a Level column with the suggested `gain_db` and the peak after it, the target editable on the page.
- `tests/test_voice_audition.py` — the level measure on a known sine and on silence, the recorded level and its backfill, the target embedded in the page.
- `specs/configuration.md`, `specs/architecture.md`, `specs/audio-sink.md`, `specs/voice-audition.md`, `specs/_index.md` — the field, the gain step, the sink note, the level and gain.
- `docs/configuration.md`, `docs/index.md`, `docs/choosing-a-backend-and-voice.md`, `tools/voice-audition/README.md`, `README.md` — the field and how to calibrate it.
